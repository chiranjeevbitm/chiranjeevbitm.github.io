For PM-JAY hospitals, digital is now mandatory.

Bihar Swasthya Suraksha Samiti · Letter No. 16

“Mandatory adoption of ABDM-Compliant HMIS” for every PM-JAY-empanelled private hospital in Bihar.

Deadline extended to 20 January 2026 (letter dated 2 February 2026). Compliance protects your Ayushman income.

Source: biswass.bihar.gov.in → Letters

  • DHIS incentive: NHA pays hospitals for ABHA-linked digital records above a monthly threshold.
  • DPDP Rules 2025: patient data must be secured, with penalties of up to ₹250 crore for failures.

      08 · Patient data security

      Patient data stays private. By design.

      Health records are the most sensitive data a hospital holds. Every part of this system keeps them encrypted, inside India, visible only to the right people, and recoverable if anything goes wrong.

      Encrypted everywhere

      TLS encryption on every connection and AES-256 encryption at rest for the database, files and backups.

      Data stays in India

      Database, report files and app servers are hosted in the Mumbai region. Nothing is stored abroad.

      Right person, right data

      Each staff member gets their own login with OTP. Roles and database row-level rules decide who sees what.

      Every access logged

      Who opened, printed or changed a record is logged, and logs are kept for at least one year.

      Backups you can rewind

      Point-in-time recovery to the second, plus a daily encrypted off-site copy. We test a restore every quarter.

      AI without data leaks

      Names and IDs are masked before any AI call. AI providers keep no data and never train on it, and a doctor approves every draft.

      Tested by outsiders

      A CERT-In-empanelled firm runs a security test (VAPT) before go-live and every year. Passwords and keys are never kept in code.

      Consent and patient rights

      Consent forms in Hindi and English, an ABDM consent manager, and simple handling of patient requests to see, correct or delete data.
