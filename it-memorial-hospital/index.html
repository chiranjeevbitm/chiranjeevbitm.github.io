02 · Why now

For PM-JAY hospitals, digital is now mandatory.

Bihar has made ABDM-compliant hospital software a condition for private hospitals that treat Ayushman patients. Getting this right protects your income, and it can also earn incentive payments.

Bihar Swasthya Suraksha Samiti · Letter No. 16

“Mandatory adoption of ABDM-Compliant HMIS” for all PM-JAY-empanelled private hospitals in Bihar.

The deadline was extended to 20 January 2026 (letter dated 2 February 2026). Industry reading of the directive: integrate with ABDM or risk de-empanelment.

Source: biswass.bihar.gov.in → Letters

NMC · 2026HMIS–ABDM integration made mandatory for medical colleges' hospitals, with a list of empanelled ABDM software to choose from.
NHA · 2026HMIS–ABDM integration announced as a mandatory requirement; the National Health Claims Exchange can speed up claim settlement.
DHIS incentiveNHA's Digital Health Incentive Scheme pays hospitals for every ABHA-linked record above a monthly threshold.
M1

ABHA at the front desk

Create or verify each patient's ABHA during registration, including Scan & Share at the counter.

M2

Link every record

Prescriptions, lab reports and discharge summaries go to the patient's ABHA as standard FHIR records, with consent.

M3

Fetch past history

With consent, pull a referred patient's earlier reports from other hospitals. Doctors see the full picture.

03 · How a digital hospital works

One patient. One record. Every department.

A hospital information system (HIS/HMIS) is a set of connected modules around a single patient ID. Select a module to see what it does and where the money is.

Cloud SaaS

Vendor hosts the software; you pay monthly. Fast to start and always up to date. Needs two internet lines.

Hybrid Recommended

Cloud HMIS with offline fallback for billing, pharmacy and front desk, built for real power and internet conditions in Bihar.

On-premise

Local server, one-time licence plus a yearly AMC. Full control, but more upkeep and hardware risk.

04 · What the market charges

The software is a commodity.

Monthly price ranges published for Indian hospital software in 2026. The spread is wide because "HMS" can mean anything from an appointment book to a full clinical, billing and insurance platform.

Sources: Approids, Cufront, ICG (2026 cost guides); Capterra (Practo Insta IP plan: $40/user/month, minimum 20 users ≈ ₹70k+); one-time licences add AMC.

Licences are cheap and getting cheaper. What tier-2 Bihar hospitals lack is local implementation, Hindi-first patient engagement on WhatsApp, and AI that saves doctors' time. That is where this proposal focuses.
