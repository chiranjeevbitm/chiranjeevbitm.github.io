Is patient data safe?

It is stored in India and encrypted. Every staff member has their own login, and every change is logged. Patients give consent before anything is sent on WhatsApp. You can export everything at any time.

I'm faster with a pen.

Most visits match one of your 10 common diagnoses. A template fills the medicines, doses and advice in one tap, and you only change what differs. Voice input does the rest.

What if the internet goes down?

The app keeps working offline and printing still works. Prescriptions sync and WhatsApp messages go out when the connection returns.

Can my compounder use it?

The reception screen only has name, age, phone and complaint. It creates the token and the patient is in your queue.
